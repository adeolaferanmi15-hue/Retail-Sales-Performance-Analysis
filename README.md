# Retail Sales Performance Analysis

## Project Story

Businesses generate large amounts of sales data every day, but raw data alone does not provide clear answers for decision-making.

In this project, I analyzed a retail sales dataset using Microsoft Excel to understand how different products, customers, and regions contribute to overall revenue. The goal was to transform raw transactional data into meaningful insights that can help guide business strategies.

This project follows a complete data analysis workflow — from understanding the business problem to cleaning the data, analyzing trends, and building a dashboard to communicate insights.

---

## Business Problem / Objective

The business wants to better understand its sales performance and identify opportunities for growth.

Key questions explored in this analysis include:

- Which products generate the most revenue?
- How do customer purchasing behaviors differ?
- Are there differences in sales performance across regions?
- What trends can be observed in the revenue data?

By answering these questions, the business can make more informed decisions about marketing, product focus, and regional strategies.

---

## Dataset Overview

The dataset contains retail transaction records including:

Each row represents a sales transaction, allowing analysis of customer purchasing patterns and product performance.

## Key Insights

Several important insights emerged from the analysis:

**Product Performance**

Technology-related products generated the highest revenue, indicating strong customer demand in this category.

**Customer Purchasing Behavior**

Corporate customers tend to make higher-value purchases compared to other customer groups.

**Regional Performance**

Sales performance varies across regions, with some areas contributing a larger share of total revenue.

**Revenue Trends**

Revenue fluctuates across different periods, suggesting potential patterns in purchasing behavior.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the findings from the analysis, the following recommendations can help improve business performance:

- Focus marketing efforts on high-performing products
- Develop strategies to improve performance in lower-performing regions
- Monitor sales trends regularly to identify peak demand periods
- Strengthen customer engagement to encourage repeat purchases
